An original finite-outcome exercise: choose one of four equally likely tickets p, q, r, s. Two different departments print the same short event label 'Ready'. In the assembly department, Ready means tickets p or q. In the inspection department, Ready means tickets q or r. These are different events despite identical printed labels. Their intersection is q; their union is p, q, r. Each event has probability 1/2, the intersection 1/4, and the union 3/4. Keep both event identities selectable so their membership can be compared. The shared word Ready does not merge departments or events.
