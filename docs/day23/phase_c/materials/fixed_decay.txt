An original mathematical teaching model uses y(t)=4*exp(-0.25*t) on 0<=t<=6 seconds. The source specifies amplitude exactly 4 units and decay rate exactly 0.25 per second. Both are fixed constants in this exercise, not uncertainty intervals and not learner-adjustable ranges. Time can move. The derived instantaneous magnitude is y(t); its rate is -0.25*y(t). At t=0, magnitude is 4 and rate -1; at t=4, magnitude is 4/e and rate -1/e. A planar point may have coordinates (t,y(t)); the same y(t) belongs to its waveform and magnitude metric.
